How this is built Papers are pulled from PubMed by entry date across virology, molecular biology, cell biology, NGS/genomics, oncology and veterinary medicine, then ranked on journal tier and keyword overlap with the fields this brief covers; publication dates can predate the indexing window. Industry items come from ten topic news feeds over the same window, de-duplicated, with stock-promotion and aggregator content dropped. “Most-shared” is the number of independent outlets that ran the same story — social platforms expose no engagement data to this pipeline, so no post-level virality is claimed.
